Engagement

“No research without action, no action without research” is an idea Kurt Lewin formulated already in 1946. My work follows this principle. I co-design field experiments with government partners and bridge research and practice through advisory roles, media engagement, public service organizations, and speaking.

Research-Practice Partnerships

I co-design field experiments with public sector organizations to ensure research directly informs policy and management decisions. My current flagship project, funded by the Carlsberg Foundation (Semper Ardens Accomplish), investigates how to sustainably recruit, select, and retain skilled international nursing staff. The project is led by principal investigator Christian B. Jacobsen, together with Diana Galos and myself at Aarhus University.

Speaking & Workshops

I regularly speak at practitioner conferences, government agencies, and academic institutions across Denmark, Germany, and internationally. I present in English, German, and Danish. For speaking inquiries, please get in touch.

AI in Public Administration

How artificial intelligence is reshaping decision-making in the public sector, including both opportunities and risks of algorithmic tools.

Evidence-Based Hiring

What works in attracting and selecting talent for public employers, drawing on large-scale field experiments with municipalities, public utilities, and healthcare organizations.

International Workforce

How to sustainably recruit, integrate, and retain international employees, with a focus on healthcare professionals and the Danish and European labor market.

Behavioral Interventions in Government

Designing and testing behavioral interventions in collaboration with government partners, from vaccination campaigns to public service delivery.

Public Leadership

Managing workforce challenges in the public sector, including retention, motivation, and the role of leaders.
